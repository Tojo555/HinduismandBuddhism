Myth Court

The case (6 pts): Name two terms (1 pt each). Tie each one to a specific thing in the story and explain how the story works as a myth through it: a ritual, an ethic, or a worldview (2 pts each).

Objections: Every other team may submit one written objection naming the point they challenge and why. If the judge sustains it, that team steals 1 point.
